## Origin section

### Doi Chaang
| Fact on page | Source |
|---|---|
| Arabica, mainly Caturra, Catimor and Catuai, on the slopes of Doi Chaang mountain, 1,100–1,700 m; Wawee (Wawi) subdistrict, Mae Suai district, Chiang Rai | EU Official Journal C 49/8 (2014), "Kafae Doi Chaang" PGI application: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/LexUriServ/LexUriServ.do?uri=OJ%3AC%3A2014%3A049%3A0008%3A0012%3AEN%3APDF |
| Shade of plum, peach, pear and macadamia trees | Same EU document |
| In 1969 hill-tribe farmers were encouraged to grow winter crops instead of opium; arabica was one of them (King Rama IX initiative) | Doi Chaang Coffee, "The Legend": https://doichaangcoffee.co.th/en/about-us/the-legend/ ; Bangkok Post, "From poppy fields to coffee plantations": https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/1345914/from-poppy-fields-to-coffee-plantations |
| Grown by Akha, Lisu and Chinese families | Doi Chaang Coffee, "The Legend" (above) |
| EU Geographical Indication registration, August 2015 | Doi Chaang Coffee, "The land": https://doichaangcoffee.co.th/en/about-us/the-land/ |

Note: the company's own site gives 1,000–1,700 m. The page uses the official EU specification range of 1,100–1,700 m.

### Doi Tung
| Fact on page | Source |
|---|---|
| Arabica hybrids (Catimor, Caturra, Catuai), Nang Non mountain range, 800–1,200 m, Mae Fah Luang and Mae Sai districts, natural forest shade | EU Official Journal C 48/14 (2014), "Kafae Doi Tung" PGI application: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/LexUriServ/LexUriServ.do?uri=OJ:C:2014:048:0014:0018:EN:PDF |
| Started 1988 under the Doi Tung Development Project to help residents switch from opium poppy to coffee; cherries sorted and processed using the washed method at a facility designated by the Foundation; UNODC accreditation in 2001 | Japan GI register, entry 138 "DoiTung Coffee": https://pd.jgic.jp/en/register/entry/138.html |
| Project initiated by the Princess Mother and run by the Mae Fah Luang Foundation under Royal Patronage; established 1988; six ethnic groups: Akha, Lahu, Shan, Tai Lue, Lua, Yunnanese | DoiTung, "About Doi Tung": https://www.doitung.com/en/about/ ; Mae Fah Luang Foundation: https://www.maefahluang.org/en/doitung-development-project/ |
| Coffee plantations about 15 km² on Doi Tung; nearly 900 families earn their income from them | CNA, "From one addiction to another: Thailand's ethnic minorities quit opium for coffee": https://www.channelnewsasia.com/asia/one-addiction-another-thailands-ethnic-minorities-quit-opium-coffee-833166 |

### Wawee
| Fact on page | Source |
|---|---|
| Mountainous, 823–1,204 m; first settled by Lahu (Muser) and Lisu, with Yao, Karen, Akha and Chin Ho arriving later; supported by the Royal Project Foundation and HRDI; main crops include coffee, tea, lychee and macadamia | Highland Research and Development Institute (HRDI), Wawee Royal Project area fact sheet: https://www.hrdi.or.th/en/en-admin/public/images/WhereWeWork/HighlandDevelopmentRoyalProjectAreas/FILE-20240319-0948J1UU8ASR4YPE.pdf |

Note: the HRDI range describes the subdistrict's terrain in general, not specifically the coffee plots.

## Menu: which drinks are popular
| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Es-yen is Thailand's most popular café coffee and a top seller in every type of coffee shop; usual recipe is 2 shots of espresso with condensed, evaporated and fresh milk; it grew out of old-style "kafae yen boran" brewed in a cloth filter | Bangkok Biz News (Thai), "เอสเย็น กาแฟซิกเนเจอร์ไทย": https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/lifestyle/food/1234241 |
| Iced americano and iced latte are the most common café orders; oliang and kafae boran were the traditional cold coffees | Coffee Intelligence, "Cold coffee reigns supreme in Thailand" (Oct 2024): https://intelligence.coffee/2024/10/in-thailand-cold-coffee-reigns-supreme/ |
| Americano, latte, cappuccino, mocha, espresso and flat white are widely available; descriptions of oliang (dark, roasted, smoky), kafae yen and es-yen; หวานน้อย / ไม่หวาน phrases | Live and Work in Thailand, "How to Order Coffee in Thailand": https://www.live-and-work-in-thailand.com/live-and-work-in-thailand-blog/how-to-order-coffee-in-thailand-street-coffee-caf-drinks-and-useful-thai-phrases |
| Thai names อเมริกาโน่, ลาเต้, คาปูชิโน่, เอสเพรสโซ่, มอคค่า, คาราเมลมัคคิอาโต (caramel macchiato on Thai café menus) | Ling, "Order Coffee in Thai": https://ling-app.com/blog/order-coffee-in-thai/ |
| Kafae boran made with condensed and evaporated milk | Healthy Thai Recipes: https://healthythairecipes.com/th/ (search result "เอสเพรสโซ่ไทยเย็น, Kafae Boran") |
| Thai menu lineup (espresso, black coffee, cappuccino, latte, mocha, macchiato) at Thailand's largest chain | Café Amazon menu, via Bangkok Menu (Jan 2025): https://www.bangkokmenu.org/cafe-amazon-menu/ |

แฟลตไวท์ (flat white) is the standard Thai transliteration, but I did not find a source for it.
